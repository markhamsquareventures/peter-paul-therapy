## What to Expect

**Subtitle:** New to therapy? Here are answers to the questions I hear most often.

### What happens in the first therapy session?

The first session is about getting to know each other. I'll ask about what brought you to therapy, what you're hoping to get out of it, and what's been going on in your life. You'll get a sense of whether this feels like a good fit. There's no pressure. Think of it as a conversation, not an evaluation.

### How long are sessions, and how often do we meet?

Sessions are either 45 or 60 minutes. Most people start weekly, and we adjust the frequency as things progress.

### Do you offer online therapy or in-person sessions in NYC?

I offer telehealth (video) sessions throughout New York State. No commute, no waiting room. You can attend from wherever you're comfortable.

### How do I know if therapy is right for me?

You don't need to be in crisis. If something in your life feels stuck or confusing and you want a space to work through it, that's enough reason.

### How do I find the right therapist in NYC?

It's about fit, not just credentials. The most important thing is whether you feel comfortable and heard. I offer a consultation so you can get a sense of what working together would be like. No pressure, no commitment.
