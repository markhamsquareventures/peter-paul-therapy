About Peter

My background is in education, philosophy, and comparative religion. Not the typical path into therapy, but it means I take the bigger questions about your life as seriously as the day-to-day struggles.

I have my MSW from Fordham and five years of clinical experience. When you sit with me, you'll find someone who listens more than they talk, asks real questions, and doesn't pretend to have all the answers. We figure it out together.

The Therapy

To support your growth, I draw from a range of evidence-based and insight-oriented approaches.

Insight-Oriented Depth

Psychodynamic and relational work to uncover the root of recurring patterns, and existential perspectives to tackle questions of meaning.

Structured Problem Solving

Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT) and solution-focused techniques to provide practical, brief interventions for immediate challenges.

Present-Moment Awareness

Mindfulness and humanistic approaches to build your capacity for living fully in the here and now.

What to Expect

New to therapy? Here are answers to the questions I hear most often.

The first session is about getting to know each other. I'll ask about what brought you to therapy, what you're hoping to get out of it, and what's been going on in your life. You'll get a sense of whether this feels like a good fit. There's no pressure. Think of it as a conversation, not an evaluation.
Sessions are either 45 or 60 minutes. Most people start weekly, and we adjust the frequency as things progress.
I offer telehealth (video) sessions throughout New York State and work in-person in Manhattan.
You don't need to be in crisis. If something in your life feels stuck or confusing and you want a space to work through it, that's enough reason.
It's about fit, not just credentials. The most important thing is whether you feel comfortable and heard. I offer a free consultation so you can get a sense of what working together would be like. No pressure, no commitment.
